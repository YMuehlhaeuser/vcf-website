Our verification research cluster is open

Researchers working on AI verification can now apply for free, bare-metal access to current datacenter GPUs from NVIDIA and AMD.

August 2026 Verifiable Compute Foundation

Many of the methods proposed for verifying international AI agreements depend on hardware access that commercial clouds don't give. Measuring what a chip is doing, reading its firmware or proving that a computation ran as declared usually requires the machine itself, with nothing in between.

Until now, most researchers working on these methods have had no way to test them on the datacenter GPUs an agreement would actually cover. Starting today, VCF's research cluster gives them that access, free of charge.

What's available

  • NVIDIA H100, H200 and B300 GPUs
  • AMD MI355X GPUs
  • Bare-metal access, with confidential computing

Who can apply

The cluster is open to researchers at universities, nonprofits and companies working on AI verification. We also run training programs that bring cohorts of people new to the field onto the cluster.
